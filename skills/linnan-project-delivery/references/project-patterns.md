# Recurring Project Patterns

These are decision patterns, not text to copy. Use only the matching section and adapt it to current evidence and the user's current request.

## Policy research and proposal writing

- Search relevant, accessible prior work and compare policy object + core problem + primary institutional tool. Use local work, Notion, or topic records where available, not as a mandatory all-systems checklist. Missing indexes do not block independent research or drafting unless the user explicitly made complete de-duplication a prerequisite. State the actual coverage; never claim comprehensive de-duplication without it. Preserve any user-required topic/preview approval before authoring.
- Focus on one policy object, one core problem, and one main institutional tool unless the user explicitly requests a broader synthesis.
- Apply [policy-writing-profile.md](policy-writing-profile.md) for source dates, structure, writing style, and default Word delivery. Separate national totals, specialist datasets, local cases, and inference.
- Treat topic candidates and research leads as candidates, not submitted, adopted, or achieved results.

## Personal courseware and履职 materials

- Foreground the user's real cases, method evolution, and verified results rather than generic biography or invented achievements.
- Keep candidate-material counts separate from formal submission, adoption, or award figures.
- For decks, keep slide-level source notes and complete render/overflow checks.
- Preserve the editable original plus a rebuild path when feasible.

## Knowledge systems and planning

- Search and synthesize existing plans before asking the user to restate them.
- Use checkable actions, owners, due windows, monthly outcomes, and review questions for operational plans.
- Put live tasks/CRM/status/contracts/quotations/payments in the operational platform; put durable knowledge and methods in the knowledge base with evidence metadata.
- Confirm parent location, permissions, and the created/updated content by re-reading it.

## Business data, ERP, and automation

- Begin with a read-only, auditable loop and the authoritative data source.
- Missing physical counts, untraceable batches, negative stock, conflicting ledgers, or ambiguous continuation remain `REVIEW`.
- Keep rules/SOPs/tests separate from dynamic ERP or database records.
- A pilot should use a dedicated scope, explicit backups or audit logs when useful, and a stop condition before production posting.

## External communication and integrations

- Draft first. For consequential messages, preserve Chinese analysis/priorities and provide bilingual drafts when the recipient context calls for it.
- Apply the approval rule in [evidence-and-authority.md](evidence-and-authority.md#authority-boundaries), then verify the actual recipient/chat/thread or authoritative Sent record after sending. Do not add a second confirmation requirement here.
- For new applications, use least privilege and enumerate remaining manual console or authorization steps.
- Verify package/source integrity and safe runtime surfaces before adding credentials, paid calls, or destructive execution.
