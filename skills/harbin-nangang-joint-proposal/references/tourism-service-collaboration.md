# Harbin all-season tourism service collaboration

Use this reference for a proposal whose single policy object is the visitor journey across transport, ticketing changes and complaint handling.

## Scope

- Keep the core institutional tool narrow: a city-level cross-department service-collaboration mechanism. Do not expand it into generic tourism promotion, construction, or seasonal coupon policy.
- Explain why Harbin needs a seasonal design: ice-and-snow peaks, summer travel, holidays, events, flight delays, snow and road conditions can all break one visitor journey.
- Separate municipal action from provincial, railway, airline or airport matters. For the latter, propose a municipal coordination list and liaison route, not a unilateral municipal mandate.

## Executable design

- Municipal cultural-tourism department coordinates; transport, market regulation, public-security traffic management, districts/counties and the 12345 route each have defined responsibilities.
- Use a visitor-journey service list covering arrival, transfer, visit, lodging and departure; publish a dynamic route table for key nodes and weather/event disruption.
- For bundled tickets or one-platform orders, require the seller to accept the first request, display linked refund/change rules before purchase, and provide one progress-query route.
- Give cross-industry complaints one case number, a lead unit, a time limit, a result notice and a review path. Do not require the visitor to repeat the same facts to several agencies.
- Preserve special routes for older visitors, families with children, disabled visitors, international visitors and emergencies such as night arrival, lost luggage, power loss, payment failure or medical need.

## Evidence and evaluation

- Use current official Harbin, Heilongjiang and central sources for factual statements about transport and tourism-service policy. Do not use tourist volumes, operating routes or a historic action plan as proof of current service status unless date and scope are verified.
- Assess pilots using arrival success, wait time, information-update timeliness, refund/change completion time, one-time complaint acceptance, response timeliness and visitor feedback; do not treat platform visits or meeting counts as outcomes.
- Pilot first on a small number of city-controlled or jointly coordinated corridors. State the resource source, data-protection boundary, annual review method and exit or adjustment rule.
